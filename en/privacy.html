1. Introduction

Welcome to Virtual Address Generator ("the Website"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our Website.

By using the Website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Website.

2. Information We Collect

When you visit the Website, we may automatically collect non-personally identifiable information through third-party services (such as Google Analytics and Google AdSense):

  • Usage Data: Your interactions on the Website, such as clicks, scrolling, and time spent on pages

2.2 Information You Provide

The core functionality of the Website runs locally in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit the address data you generate. All address generation and saving functionality is implemented through browser local storage (localStorage), and data is stored only on your device.

2.3 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, unless you voluntarily provide it through our contact form)

  • Precise geolocation information

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

3.1 What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help remember your preferences, improve user experience, and analyze website traffic.

3.2 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Ensure basic website functionality, such as language preference settings
  • Analytics Cookies: Through tools like Google Analytics to understand how users interact with the Website

3.3 Managing Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality. You can also visit Google Ad Settings to manage personalized ad preferences.
